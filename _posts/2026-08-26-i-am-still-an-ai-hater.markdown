I mean, you're not surprised, right?  

When I posted [I Am An AI Hater](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2025/08/26/i-am-an-ai-hater.html) I did not expect it to reach so many people. I am grateful to everyone who has shared it online and offline or who messaged me to say that it captured what they felt. A year later, the bad news is that AI remains an active threat, a dangerous political project, and a mountain of horrendous bullshit. The good news is that many people hate it. 
<br>
<br>

### Translations

I am surprised, delighted, and grateful at how many people reached out wanting to translate "I Am an AI Hater" into other languages. It has now been translated into ten languages besides the original English: 
[Brazilian Portuguese](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2025/12/29/eu-odeio-ia.html), [Italian](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2026/03/20/odio-l'ia.html), [Spanish](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2026/05/07/odio-la-ia.html), [French](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2026/05/08/je-hais-l'ia.html), [Russian](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2026/05/09/%D0%AF-%D0%BD%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%B6%D1%83-%D0%98%D0%98.html), [Finnish](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2026/05/09/vihaan-tekoa-lya.html), [Latin](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2026/05/18/intellegentiae-artificialis-osor-sum.html), [Catalan](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2026/06/19/soc-un-odiador-de-la-ia.html), [German](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2026/06/19/ich-bin-ein-ki-hasser.html), and [Dutch](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2026/08/22/ik-ben-een-ai-hater.html).

I like to think of these great people as "The International AI Haters Union," and I have compiled their efforts into a PDF: [The International AI Hater's Union, Volume 1](https://storage.googleapis.com/pdt_central/frameshop/The%20International%20AI%20Haters%20Union%2C%20vol%201.pdf). If you speak a language that is not included in volume 1, please feel free to translate it and send it to me for inclusion in future editions.

### Podcasts 
If you like podcasts, I recommend:
- [Mystery AI Hype Theater 3000](https://dair-institute.org/maiht3k/)  
in which Prof. Emily M Bender and sociologist Dr. Alex Hanna provide an ongoing stream of "oh my god this is all bullshit" that mostly focuses on deconstructing the rhetoric of ai hypemongers by making fun of badly formed artifacts. 

- [Politechs](https://politechs.dev)  
Josh and Ray are software developers talking to a lot of sharp guests ([including me](politechs.dev/episodes/s02-hate/)) about ai and its implications

- [Tech Won't Save Us](https://www.techwontsave.us)  
Paris Marx is a good interviewer, check out the [Data Vampires](https://www.techwontsave.us/data-vampires/) mini-series


- [The Data Fix](https://shows.acast.com/the-data-fix)  
critical media studies scholar Mél Hogan has really good conversations with really interesting people
